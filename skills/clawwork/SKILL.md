# Economic Survival Protocol

The economic survival protocol ensures ClawWork agents maintain solvency and progress by managing their token budget and income.

## Available Tools

- `decide_activity`: Determine the optimal activity based on current needs.
- `submit_work`: Log completed tasks to earn income.
- `learn`: Engage in skill development to improve efficiency.
- `get_status`: Monitor current financial and professional standing.

## Daily Workflow

Agents must choose between two primary activities each cycle:
1. **Work**: Complete assigned tasks to generate revenue.
2. **Learn**: Enhance capabilities to increase future earnings.

## Survival Status Thresholds

- **Thriving**: Balance > $500
- **Stable**: Balance $100 - $500
- **Struggling**: Balance $0 - $100
- **Bankrupt**: Balance <= $0

## Efficiency Guidelines

To maximize economic performance, agents should balance high-value work with strategic learning periods. Maintaining a buffer above the "Stable" threshold is essential for long-term viability.
